About MindMetric

A softer way to look at
how daily life affects well-being

Students juggle study, social media, sleep and stress every single day, and it is hard to see how those pieces fit together. MindMetric turns twelve simple answers about your routine into a single, easy-to-read well-being score.

The score comes from a scikit-learn regression model trained on a dataset of student social-media and mental-health survey responses. It is served through a FastAPI backend, so every score you see is computed live by the model.

Please note: MindMetric is an educational, ML-generated estimate. It is not a clinical diagnosis and cannot replace advice from a qualified professional.

How it works

From a few answers to a clear score

Four simple steps, and the whole thing takes about two minutes.

  1. 1

    Share your routine

    Tell us about your age, studies, social-media habits, sleep, activity and how stressed you feel.

  2. 2

    Answers are checked

    Each field is validated in your browser first, so values stay within sensible ranges before anything is sent.

  3. 3

    The model predicts

    Your answers go to the FastAPI backend, where the trained model estimates a well-being score from 0 to 10.

  4. 4

    See your result

    You get a gauge, a plain-language interpretation and a few gentle things to reflect on.
